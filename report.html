2015Recovery

Bear-Market Bottom & Accumulation

After the 2013 Mt. Gox hack wiped 850 000 BTC and trust in exchanges, Bitcoin spent 2015 grinding near $200–400. Long-term believers accumulated quietly. The Bitcoin Core dev community grew, laying technical foundations for the years ahead.

$170 $430
Jul 2016Halving #2

Second Block-Reward Halving

The mining reward cut from 25 → 12.5 BTC per block. Historically, halvings reduce supply growth and precede major bull markets by 12–18 months. Institutional interest in blockchain began attracting venture capital to the space for the first time.

$650 $780
2017Bull Run

ICO Mania — First Mainstream Bubble

Ethereum's smart contracts enabled Initial Coin Offerings (ICOs) — anyone could raise millions in hours. Bitcoin rode the euphoria from $1 000 in January to $19 783 in December. CNBC, Reddit, and kitchen-table conversations drove retail FOMO to historic extremes. Google searches for "buy bitcoin" peaked globally.

2018−84%

Crypto Winter — ICO Collapse

Regulators worldwide cracked down on unregistered securities (ICOs). Most altcoin projects delivered nothing. The Bitcoin futures market launched on CME allowed institutional short-selling. BTC fell from $19 k to a low of $3 200 by December — an 84% drawdown that wiped out millions of late retail investors.

2019–2020Recovery

Libra Shock & COVID Flash Crash

Facebook's Libra announcement pushed BTC to $13 800 — the biggest single catalyst since 2017. Then COVID-19 in March 2020 caused a global risk-off panic: BTC crashed 52% in a single day to $3 850, testing the resolve of hodlers. The Fed's historic money-printing response set the stage for Bitcoin's next supercycle.

May 2020Halving #3

Third Halving — Institutional Supercycle

Reward halved to 6.25 BTC. This cycle was different: MicroStrategy put $425M of corporate treasury into BTC, Tesla bought $1.5B, PayPal enabled 300M users to buy Bitcoin, and Grayscale accumulated billions. For the first time, Wall Street and corporate America treated BTC as a legitimate inflation hedge.

May 2021−53%

China Mining Ban & Elon Twitter Effect

Elon Musk reversed Tesla's BTC payment acceptance, citing energy concerns. China's government banned Bitcoin mining — forcing ~65% of the global hashrate to relocate overnight. BTC crashed from $64 k to $29 000. Yet the network survived and hashrate recovered fully within months, proving decentralisation's resilience.

2022−77%

LUNA/UST Collapse & FTX Bankruptcy

Two catastrophic black-swan events defined 2022. In May, the Terra/LUNA ecosystem imploded — a $60B algorithmic stablecoin project collapsed to zero in 72 hours, wiping out retail and institutional investors. In November, FTX — the world's second-largest exchange — filed bankruptcy amid a $8B fraud, triggering a contagion that crashed BTC to $15 700. Industry credibility hit a historic low.

Jan 2024ETF Approved

Spot Bitcoin ETF — Wall Street Goes All-In

After a decade of rejections, the SEC approved 11 spot Bitcoin ETFs simultaneously, including BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust. On day one, trading volume exceeded $4.6B — the largest ETF launch in history. Within 3 months, these ETFs accumulated over 500 000 BTC ($35B+), dwarfing any previous institutional accumulation event.

Fourth Halving — Reward Drops to 3.125 BTC

The fourth halving cut the block reward to 3.125 BTC. For the first time, a halving occurred after a new all-time high had already been set — traditionally ATHs came after halvings. The combination of ETF demand and supply reduction created unprecedented buy-side pressure.

Key Takeaways

01

Halvings Are the Metronome

Every ~4 years, supply growth halves. Each post-halving cycle has produced a new ATH, though with diminishing percentage returns as market cap grows.

02

Fear Kills More Portfolios Than Crashes

Investors who panic-sold at every 50%+ drawdown locked in losses. Those who held through all four cycles turned $1 000 into $323 000+ — a 32 300% return.

03

Each Cycle Has a Unique Catalyst

2013: geeks & libertarians. 2017: ICO speculation. 2020–21: institutional adoption. 2024: ETF legitimisation. Narratives evolve, but supply scarcity is constant.

04

Macro Conditions Matter More Now

With $35B+ in ETF AUM and corporate treasuries holding BTC, Bitcoin now correlates more with risk assets during crises — but diverges during liquidity expansions.
